Privacy Policy — The Last Bell

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game The Last Bell (“the game”) handles. The game is published by BlairsLab (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. The game does not send your progress or anything else to us.
  • The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not collect, share or sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

So you can continue playing, the game saves on your device: your progress (runs, towers, Bell-metal, Foundry upgrades and best wave), which first-time hints you have seen, and your settings. This information stays in the game’s private storage on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the game. The game does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or similar permissions, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports through its Android vitals service, depending on your device settings. These do not identify you.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your progress. We cannot recover it.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Australia’s Privacy Act 1988, New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing on our systems to access or delete, but we are happy to answer questions at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

6. Changes to this policy

If a later version of the game adds optional features that handle information (such as optional video ads, purchases or leaderboards), we will update this page and its date before that version is released, and note the change in the game’s update notes.
